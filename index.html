♡ FASHION GUIDE ♡

Cute Outfit Ideas for School 🎀

Getting dressed for school doesn't have to be boring. Here are a few easy ideas to make your everyday outfits feel cute, comfortable and completely you.

🎀 Keep it simple

Start with your favourite jeans, skirt or trousers and add a cute top. Small details like earrings, hair accessories or a pretty bag can change the whole look.

🩷 Pick a colour theme

Try choosing two or three colours that look good together.

✨ Add your signature accessory

A bow, bracelet, necklace, cute socks or favourite hair clip can become your little signature.

☁️ Comfort comes first

The cutest outfit is one you actually feel comfortable wearing.

♡ BEAUTY GUIDE ♡

Your Simple Glow-Up Routine 💄✨

A glow-up isn't about becoming someone else. It's about taking care of yourself and feeling confident in your own skin.

🫧 Start with a fresh face

Wash your face gently and keep your skincare routine simple.

🎀 Take care of your hair

Brush or style your hair in a way that makes you feel pretty.

💗 Look after yourself

Drink some water, get enough rest and give yourself time to relax.

✨ Add your little finishing touches

Try your favourite lip balm, perfume, jewellery or accessory.

♡ COZY LIFE GUIDE ♡

How to Make an Ordinary Day Feel Special ☕✨

You don't need a special occasion to make your day feel magical.

☕ Make yourself a cozy drink

Make a cup of hot chocolate, tea, coffee or your favourite drink.

🎀 Put on an outfit you love

Wear something that makes you feel comfortable and cute.

🎧 Create a little playlist

Play songs that match your mood.

🕯️ Make your space cozy

Tidy one small area and make your space feel a little calmer.

📖 Give yourself a slow moment

Read, journal, watch something comforting or enjoy some quiet time.

♡ SELF-CARE GUIDE ♡

Your Gentle Reset Checklist 🧴✨

Sometimes you just need a little reset. Start small, slow down and give yourself some time to breathe.

🫧 Freshen up

Take a shower, wash your face, brush your teeth and put on comfortable clothes.

🧹 Tidy one little space

Pick one small area, put things away and make your space feel a little calmer.

💧 Take care of your body

Drink some water, eat something nourishing and give yourself a chance to rest.

📱 Take a little break

Put your phone down for a few minutes, listen to music, stretch or journal.

🎀 Do one thing just for you

Read a book, watch your favourite show or do something that makes you happy.

♡ GIRL TALK ♡

Little Reminders for Growing Confidence 🦋✨

Confidence doesn't mean feeling perfect all the time. It's about learning to appreciate yourself and trust yourself.

💗 You don't have to be perfect

You don't need to look or act perfect to be worthy of kindness and respect.

🦋 Stop comparing your journey

Focus on your own little wins and the things that make you happy.

🎀 Celebrate the little things

Those little moments count too.

✨ Talk to yourself kindly

Speak to yourself the way you would speak to a friend.

🌷 Be yourself

Your style, interests and personality don't have to match anyone else's.

♡ Y2K STYLE GUIDE ♡

Build Your Own Signature Aesthetic 💿✨

Your aesthetic doesn't have to fit into one perfect category. Mix the things you love and create a style that feels completely like you.

💿 Find your favourite colours

Think about the colours you naturally gravitate towards.

🦋 Mix cute details

Try bows, butterfly clips, glossy accessories, cute bags, jewellery or fun hair accessories.

🎧 Create your vibe

Your music, room, clothes and favourite things can all inspire your aesthetic.

📸 Make a moodboard

Save pictures of outfits, rooms, hairstyles, colours and little details that inspire you.

💗 Don't be afraid to change

Your aesthetic can change whenever you want.
